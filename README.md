# Until the Day Dawns

An 8-bit RPG through the end times. Walk with the angel Gabriel from Babel to Babylon, Galilee to the throne of heaven, and the dawn of Christ's return. You fight lies by answering questions about what you've learned, and you collect the whole armour of God along the way.

**Play:** https://mingjunko.github.io/UntilTheDayDawns/

- Works on phones (on-screen D-pad) and desktops (arrow keys / WASD, Z or Enter, X or Esc).
- Progress saves in your own browser.
- On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" to play full screen.

## Source

Based on Ps Daniel Foo's four-part End Times series, as summarised in the study companion "Until the Day Dawns". Lines marked ✦ are his own reading rather than a plain statement of Scripture. A study note in the game's Scroll menu sets out where other Christians read things differently.

Scripture quotations are from the King James Version.
